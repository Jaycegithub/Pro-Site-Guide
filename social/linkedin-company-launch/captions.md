```
Is your firm's website one weak password away from a breach? 🔐

5 fixes worth doing this week:
1. Give every person their own login, with the lowest role that works
2. Use long, unique passwords in a password manager
3. Turn on two-factor authentication for every admin
4. Keep WordPress, plugins and themes updated, and delete what you don't use
5. Back up files and the database, then test a restore

The full 21-item checklist, including what ABA Formal Opinions 477R and 483 expect, is linked in the comments 👇
```
