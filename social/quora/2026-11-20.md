**Question:** Is Jetpack Boost worth it?
**Link:** https://www.quora.com/Is-Jetpack-Boost-worth-it
**Article linked:** https://prositeguide.com/jetpack-review/
**Why this one:** evergreen question with existing views, and the Jetpack review covers Boost free vs paid directly.

## Answer (copy and paste)

```
The free version of Jetpack Boost is worth trying on most WordPress sites. The paid version is only worth it if you've already fixed the bigger speed problems and want the critical CSS handled automatically.

What the free plugin gives you:
- Optimized CSS loading and deferred non-essential JavaScript
- Page caching
- An image guide that flags oversized images
- Performance scores so you can see before/after

What paying adds: automated critical CSS (on the free version you regenerate it manually after design changes), performance history and priority support.

Before paying for anything, check the things that usually matter more: your hosting's server response time, the size of the big image at the top of your homepage, and how many third-party scripts (chat widgets, trackers) load on every page. Test your mobile score in PageSpeed Insights before and after you turn Boost on.

One caveat: don't stack Boost's page cache on top of another caching plugin or your host's built-in cache without testing. Two caches can conflict and cause stale pages.

I wrote a longer review of Jetpack, including what's free, what Boost and the paid plans add, and who should actually pay: https://prositeguide.com/jetpack-review/ (Disclosure: it's my site.)
```
