## 9. Every upload checklist

1. Title with the keyword first
2. Description from your template, with the article link in the first 2 lines
3. Chapters (00:00 Intro, etc.) for long videos
4. Custom thumbnail
5. Add to the right playlist
6. End screen (last 20 s): "Subscribe" + "Best for viewer" video
7. Cards: link to a related video around the middle
8. Pin a comment with the article link and a question
9. Share to Facebook, LinkedIn and Pinterest the same day
10. Reply to every comment in the first 24 hours
