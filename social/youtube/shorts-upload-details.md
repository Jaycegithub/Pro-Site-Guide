# YouTube Shorts: upload details

Both videos are vertical 1080×1920 and under 60 seconds, so YouTube treats them as Shorts automatically.

Upload from the **YouTube app** (tap **+** → Upload a video) or from **YouTube Studio** on a computer (Create → Upload videos). The app lets you pick the cover frame and add music; Studio is easier for pasting long descriptions.

Note: on YouTube there's no automatic DM like ManyChat. When people comment the keyword, reply to their comment with the link yourself (YouTube allows links in replies from the channel owner, though some may be held for review). The link is also in the description and the pinned comment, so nobody has to wait.

## Settings for both

| Setting | Choose |
|---|---|
| Audience | No, it's not made for kids |
| Visibility | Public (or Schedule, see below) |
| Playlist | Quick Website Tips (Shorts) |
| Category | Education |
| Comments | On |
| Language | English |
| Altered or synthetic content | YouTube asks this mainly for realistic content people could mistake for real (a real person's face or voice). Your videos use a cartoon character and a generic AI narrator, so "No" is usually fine. If you'd rather be cautious, ticking "Yes" doesn't reduce reach. |
| Music | Optional: add a quiet track in the YouTube app (10–15% volume) so the voice stays clear |

## When to post
- Don't upload both at once. Post **Short 1 today** and **Short 2 in 2–3 days**, so each gets its own push.
- Best time for a US audience: **8–10 PM Philippine time** (morning in the US).
- Reply to every comment in the first 24 hours.
