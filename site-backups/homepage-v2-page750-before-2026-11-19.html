Which hosting should I choose for a small business site?⌄
Look past the intro price: compare renewal cost, backup frequency, staging and support. Our Hostinger vs SiteGround comparison and hosting guide show the trade-offs side by side.
Do I need WordPress, or is a website builder enough?⌄
Builders like Squarespace or Wix are quick to start; WordPress gives you more control, plugins and ownership. See Squarespace vs WordPress vs Wix to decide.
